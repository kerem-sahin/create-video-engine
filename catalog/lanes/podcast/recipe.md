# Lane: podcast (voice to clips)

A show's audio turned into vertical clips whose picture **tells what is said**, in the show's own look. Read after `make` step 0. Full-episode videos are not in this lane yet: say so if asked.

## Setup extras: the show already taught everything

Ask for whatever exists (the show's link or RSS feed, a folder of past recordings, the website, the cover) and read it before any question:

1. `node parts/show-reader/show-reader.mjs read <link|feed|folder> --out brands/<show>/sources/show.json`. Exit 1: say what is missing (usually the RSS link, which every hosting dashboard shows). A folder gives no titles: ask for them, never use file names.
2. Ask before downloading past episodes (how many, how many minutes): `download --show … --dir brands/<show>/sources/episodes`.
3. `node parts/memory/memory.mjs transcribe --show … --memory brands/<show>/memory`, then `index`. Fill `memory/CHANNEL.md` from the transcript lines around each candidate, every line with a citation. Show the person a short "this is how I understood your show" and correct it with their words.
4. The look comes from the show's own material (cover drawing style, site, title wording), never from another show. Offer the two palettes (`setup`). Ask once how the people who talk appear: drawn characters in the cover's style (from photos they give, or described), their nicknames and a simple sign, or not at all. Names on screen only as the person says; a third person's name never. Draw the characters once, carefully, show them to the person, and keep the approved ones in `brands/<show>/library/` (`library.json`, `approved: true`): every clip reuses them, so the show has a face.

## A clip

0. **Create the project first** (`make` step 3: `hyperframes init` refuses a folder that already has files), then cut into its `assets/`.
1. **Pick the moment** with the person (`node parts/moments/moments.mjs` proposes; they choose), 20–45 s, starting on a sentence. **The hook**: a stranger scrolling decides in two seconds. The clip opens on a line that stands alone and makes them want the rest (a question, a claim, a laugh). If the best line comes late, open on it (a 2–3 s cold open of the punchline with its drawing), then go back to the start; tell the person you did. Never open on small talk.
2. **Cut it**: `node parts/assemble-long/assemble-long.mjs slice --audio <episode audio> --from <s> --to <s> --out-dir <dir>/assets` (never splits a word, snaps to sentences).
3. **Transcribe the clip itself**: `node parts/transcribe-local/transcribe.mjs <dir>/assets/clip.m4a --language <code> --out <dir>/assets/words.json` (default model `medium`). Never reuse a weaker episode transcript for timings: one skipped sentence put a whole clip 3 s early. Check every name, brand and place in the clip against `CHANNEL.md` and fix its spelling (a car brand heard as "kupra" is written "Cupra"), and fix other mishearings you are sure of, by editing the word text only, never the times; list every fix and every unsure word for the person; an unsure word never goes on screen.
4. **Plan the picture**: first list every concrete thing said in the clip (objects, money, places, animals, food, a gesture), then for each sentence what is drawn: the thing said, the punchline as big type when it lands, the speaker's character reacting. A concrete thing that is said and not drawn needs a reason (too fast, a third person's private detail); only names and captions on screen feels empty (a real person's first clip: "para geçince ekranda para çıksa"). Something changes every 2–4 s and always on the word it shows, and a scroller must notice it at phone size: a new drawing, a big move, a scene that builds. A stroke added to the same two drawings is not a change; if the talk circles one idea for 10 s or more, find what else was said (a place, a person's reaction, an example) and draw that (a real clip held the same two drawings for 30 s). Look in `brands/<show>/library/` first. A named real thing (a Porsche 911, not "a car") is drawn from a real photo: `node parts/draw-from-photo/draw-from-photo.mjs find "<thing> side view" --out <dir>/assets/refs/<slug>`, look at the photos, then `ink … --library brands/<show>/library --name <slug> --what "<words>" --credits <dir>/CREDITS.md` in the show's ink and paper colours; look at the drawing and pick another photo if anything is missing or blotched (its README). Nothing usable found: ask the person for their own photo (the original file, not a screenshot: small photos draw blotchy). A photo they took from the web has unknown rights: say so in one sentence, let them decide, and write it in the credit. Do what `ink`'s `look out:` lines say before showing a drawing. Then check the frame for one hand: characters and objects at the same line weight and detail.
5. **Build** in HyperFrames: the clip audio as a root `<audio>` at 0, the drawings in the show's line style, captions from `words.json`. The end card: the show's name and where to listen. HyperFrames' mix makes speech about 3 dB louder than the source file: set the `<audio>` `data-volume` from verify's loudness on the first draft (−16 LUFS ±1), not from measuring the clip.
6. **Check timing**: `node parts/transcribe-local/sync-check.mjs <draft.mp4> --words <dir>/assets/words.json --language <code>`. Exit 1: the picture and captions are early or late; fix before anyone sees it. A few single words 0.5–0.9 s off on the compressed render while the median is near 0 are the transcriber's jitter, not a shift; run it on the clip audio too if unsure. Then verify and the fresh critic as in `make` (give the critic the words spoken at each frame).

## Do not

- Do not show third persons' names or private details said in passing.
- Do not invent: every word on screen was said; every drawn thing was mentioned.
- Do not cut laughter or the hosts' running jokes out of a clip; they are the show.
